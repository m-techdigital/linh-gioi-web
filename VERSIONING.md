## Versioning model

- `WEB-00` locks program governance only.
- `WEB-01` scaffolds the monorepo foundation.
- `WEB-02` through `WEB-07` establish design system, public site, content architecture, public RC source, portal shell and ops shell.
- `WEB-RUNTIME-PRESEED` / `WEB-RUNTIME-BROWSER-E2E-MATRIX` provide guardrails and are not the product focus.
- `WEB-PUBLIC-UX-CONTENT-POLISH-v1.6` starts actual public product polish.
- `WEB-PUBLIC-VISUAL-RESPONSIVE-POLISH-v1.7` improves visual hierarchy and responsive page composition.
- `WEB-PUBLIC-GAME-INFO-DEPTH-v1.8` deepens world story, beginner guide, download/status wording, support FAQ and community readiness.
- `WEB-PUBLIC-NEWS-GUIDE-DETAIL-PAGES-v1.9` adds richer news/guide detail pages.
- `WEB-PUBLIC-STATUS-DOWNLOAD-TRUST-POLISH-v1.10` clarifies checksum/provenance/status trust.
- `WEB-PUBLIC-COMMUNITY-ROADMAP-ONBOARDING-v1.11` connects community expectations, roadmap gates and staged release messaging.
- `WEB-PUBLIC-CONTENT-IA-HUB-POLISH-v1.12` adds the `/start` hub, content route groups and entry-question routing.
- `WEB-PUBLIC-WORLD-GAMEPLAY-LOOP-DEPTH-v1.13` adds `/game/loop`, world gameplay loop depth and gameplay boundaries.
- `WEB-PUBLIC-PLAYER-SAFETY-SUPPORT-FAQ-POLISH-v1.14` adds `/support/safety`, player safety and support expectations.
- `WEB-PUBLIC-ACCESSIBILITY-READABILITY-POLISH-v1.15` adds `/accessibility`, skip-to-content affordance and readability/focus-order guidance.
- `WEB-PUBLIC-PERFORMANCE-COPY-ASSET-BUDGET-POLISH-v1.16` adds `/performance`, copy budget, CSS-only visual budget, static route composition, perceived-load clarity and mobile density guidance.
- `WEB-PUBLIC-FAQ-SEARCH-AND-HELPFULNESS-POLISH-v1.21` adds `/support/help`, FAQ discovery groups, issue-category routing and no-search-backend helpfulness copy.
