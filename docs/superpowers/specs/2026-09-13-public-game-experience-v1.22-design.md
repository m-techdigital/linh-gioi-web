# LGO Web v1.22 — Public Game Experience & Brand Realignment

## Goal
Transform the public site from a development/status-heavy portal into a professional player-facing website whose primary story is the Linh Giới Online 2D fantasy, world, five class paths, narrative and social-action gameplay loop.

## Creative source of truth
The website derives its player-facing structure from the approved 2D scenario/design material: `LGO-2D-SCENARIO-PRODUCTION-SPINE`, `LGO-2D-SOCIAL-ACTION-DIRECTION-LOCK`, `LGO-GAME-SYSTEMS-NARRATIVE-DESIGN`, `LINH-THANH-ONBOARDING-DESIGN`, GDD and art direction. Game source/runtime state is used only to keep availability/release claims honest.

## Experience spine
1. Enter a modern spiritual world through Linh Thành.
2. Choose one of five paths: Võ, Kiếm, Pháp, Cơ, Linh.
3. Learn movement and class action at Đông Môn.
4. Leave the social hub for Linh Lâm and the outer zones.
5. Discover the fractures connecting the world to Âm Giới.
6. Join other players during Âm Giới Xâm Lăng.
7. Return to Linh Thành to socialize, progress and shape identity.

## Information architecture
Primary navigation becomes player-oriented: Home, World, Classes, Story, Journey, Guides, News, Community, Download. Release/status/support remain available as trust/support utilities, not the brand story.

## Homepage
The homepage must lead with fantasy and atmosphere, not WEB version markers. It contains: cinematic hero, three product pillars, five paths, world route, story teaser, twenty-minute session loop, signature world event, and a restrained availability CTA.

## Visual direction
Dark navy/night-spirit base, spirit cyan active light, warm gold ornament, jade support accents and shadow purple threat language. Use CSS-built parallax/depth motifs, gates, skyline, portal cracks, lantern-like glow and thin ornamental frames. No pixel-art framing and no production-art claim.

## Claims boundary
Do not imply that every planned world region, social system, class feature or public build is currently playable. Product fantasy may describe the designed universe; availability/release pages retain explicit current-state boundaries.

## Scope
Create professional player-facing content/data, new `/classes` and `/story` pages, redesign Home/Game/Journey/Start/Guides presentation, update navigation/metadata/sitemap, and add v1.22 validation/governance.

## Non-goals
No backend, auth, DB, CMS, launcher, deployment, payment/economy integration, or game-runtime implementation.
