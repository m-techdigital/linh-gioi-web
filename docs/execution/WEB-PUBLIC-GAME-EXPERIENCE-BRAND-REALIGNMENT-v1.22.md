# WEB-PUBLIC-GAME-EXPERIENCE-BRAND-REALIGNMENT-v1.22

## Goal
Make the official public site feel like Linh Giới Online first and a development portal second.

## Creative source
Player-facing hierarchy follows the approved 2D scenario/design: Linh Thành as social heart, five Lộ, side-scrolling action, progression, the Đông Môn onboarding route, the first three narrative chapters and Âm Giới Xâm Lăng.

Game runtime/source status is only used to keep availability claims honest. It does not dictate the website's brand story or page hierarchy.

## Implemented public experience
- Cinematic homepage hero: `Sống một đời khác trong Linh Giới`.
- Three pillars: Social MMORPG, Action, Progression.
- Five Lộ: Võ, Kiếm, Pháp, Cơ, Linh.
- World route: Linh Thành → Đông Môn → Linh Lâm → Cổ Di Tích → Âm Giới.
- Story route with Chapter 01–03.
- Dedicated `/classes` and `/story` pages.
- Player-journey `/journey` reframed around a 20-minute game session.
- `/start` reframed around Gate Keeper → Training Stone → movement → class skill → Shadow Slime → unlock Linh Thành.
- Primary navigation simplified to player routes; release/status/support remain trust utilities.
- CSS-only cinematic and parallax-inspired visual language using dark navy, spirit cyan, warm gold, jade and shadow purple.

## Product boundaries
- The site may describe designed game fantasy and world direction.
- It must not imply that every designed region/system is currently publicly playable.
- No public build/download artifact is claimed until Download/Status says one exists.
- No backend/auth/DB/CMS/deployment implementation is part of v1.22.

## Validator transition
Historical v1.6–v1.21 validators remain evidence for the versions they closed, but their page-composition requirements are superseded by the v1.22 player-facing IA. Core architecture/safety validators remain active and the new v1.22 validator becomes authoritative for current public presentation.
