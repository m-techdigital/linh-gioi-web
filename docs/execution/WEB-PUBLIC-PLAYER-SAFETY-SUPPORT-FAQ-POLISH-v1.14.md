# WEB-PUBLIC-PLAYER-SAFETY-SUPPORT-FAQ-POLISH-v1.14

## Product focus

WEB v1.14 continues public web development by improving player-facing safety/support communication. Runtime/browser/e2e is guardrail only.

## Implemented surfaces

- `/support/safety` safety/support hub.
- Player safety principles for privacy-first feedback.
- Support issue paths that map issues to safe reading routes.
- Closed-test support expectations before download or test access is opened.
- Community conduct rules written in friendly public language.
- News/guide content for player safety and support FAQ polish.

## Boundaries

- No live support ticket.
- No secure ticket inbox.
- No account lookup.
- No account recovery.
- No production support SLA.
- No moderation dashboard.
- No forum/chat/guild live backend.
- No fake waitlist.
- No production auth, DB persistence or backend integration.

## Validation focus

- Safety/support content exists and is route-linked.
- Guidance avoids collecting secrets, payment data, credentials, private tokens or sensitive personal data.
- Closed-test support is framed as planned/blocked until artifact, checksum, known limitations, support owner and privacy rule are approved.
- Community conduct remains static guidance until backend contracts exist.
