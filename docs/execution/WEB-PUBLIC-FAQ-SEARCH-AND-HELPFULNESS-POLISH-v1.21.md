# WEB-PUBLIC-FAQ-SEARCH-AND-HELPFULNESS-POLISH-v1.21

## Goal

Build the actual public web product by improving FAQ discoverability, helpfulness grouping, issue-category routing, no-search-backend copy and route-level support clarity.

Runtime/browser/e2e remains a guardrail only.

## Implemented surfaces

- `/support/help` as a static FAQ/helpfulness hub.
- FAQ discovery groups for download, closed test, safety/support, account/backend boundaries and gameplay expectation.
- Helpful answer prompts so FAQ copy gives a next step and avoids release/download/tester-slot claims.
- Issue-category routing that tells players what to prepare and what not to share.
- No-search-backend notes that keep the boundary explicit: no search backend, AI support bot, live ticket routing, secure inbox or SLA.

## Boundaries

- No live search backend.
- No AI support bot.
- No ticket routing backend.
- No secure ticket inbox.
- No account lookup/recovery.
- No production support SLA.
- No collection of passwords, tokens, payment data or sensitive personal data.
